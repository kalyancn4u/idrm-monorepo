# Emergency Operations Centre 101

> *Type: Guide (101 / foundational) · Audience: all stakeholders → developers · Status: Domain — phase-neutral · Track: Domain (#3)*
> *Read [Disaster Management 101](disaster-management-101.md) and [Incident Management 101](incident-management-101.md)
> first. This guide explains the "control room" of a disaster response — and how IDRM is a digital version of it.*

---

## 1. What an EOC is

An **Emergency Operations Centre (EOC)** is the place — physical or virtual — where coordinators gather
information, make decisions, and direct resources during a disaster. Think of it as **mission control**: not the
people doing rescues in the field, but the team that sees the whole picture and decides *what happens where*.

The EOC's job is **coordination**, not front-line action. It answers: what's happening, what's needed, what do we
have, and who goes where?

---

## 2. The core functions

An EOC typically covers a handful of functions (they mirror the Incident Command System — see
[Incident Command 101](incident-command-101.md)):

- **Situational awareness** — maintaining the shared picture (the [COP](common-operational-picture-101.md)).
- **Operations** — directing the response.
- **Planning** — anticipating next needs.
- **Logistics** — sourcing and moving resources.
- **Communications** — keeping everyone informed ([Emergency Communications 101](emergency-communications-101.md)).

---

## 3. How IDRM is a digital EOC

IDRM puts EOC capability into software so coordination isn't tied to one room or whiteboard:

| EOC function | In IDRM |
|---|---|
| Situational awareness | the map/COP view of live incidents & resources |
| Operations | coordinator/admin approving, assigning, verifying |
| Logistics | the **resources** module (needs ↔ means) |
| Communications | the **alerts** and **notifications** modules |
| Record & learn | the **audit** trail → After-Action Review |

The **coordinator/admin** role *is* the EOC operator in IDRM. In the MVP this is a lean web dashboard; in **FFP**
it grows into a rich, real-time Common Operational Picture with live tasking and multi-agency views.
