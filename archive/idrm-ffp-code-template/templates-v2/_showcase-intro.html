What this is, in one screen

v1 organised pages by screen. v2 organises them by role × capability — driven by the Permission Matrix below. Three new atoms make the RBAC visible on every page: a render-tier badge (who renders it), a scope banner (what data the role sees), and a [ROLE] badge by the brand. Actions a role lacks are absent from the markup — mirroring that the server simply won't render them.

The render decision (FastAPI vs Bun) — the headline

FastAPI (F) renders anything whose bytes change with who is logged in — role, scope, or privacy redaction — or that needs DB/PostGIS/Python. Per-user, barely cacheable, security-critical. RBAC + PII redaction happen where the data lives, never in the browser.

Bun (B) serves anything identical for everyone — public/static pages, assets, cluster-tile cache — plus edge plumbing (JWT verify/refresh, rate-limit, CORS, compression) and WebSocket real-time. Fast on 2G.

Hybrid (H) = a Bun-cached shell streamed instantly, with a FastAPI-rendered personalised island stitched in (htmx / ESI / streamed SSR). Fast first paint and authoritative content.

Litmus test: bytes differ by who's logged in, or hide anything a lesser role mustn't see → F. Same for everyone, or plumbing → B. Fast shell + private filling → H. Must update without reload → WS.
