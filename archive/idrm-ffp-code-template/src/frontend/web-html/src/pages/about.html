About IDRM

Integrated Disaster Response Management is a government-backed, map-driven platform that connects citizens in crisis with the people who can help — NGOs, hospitals, and volunteers — and coordinates the whole response in one place.

Who it's for

  • • Citizens — request help and track it to completion.
  • • Providers (NGOs, hospitals, volunteer groups) — accept and deliver services.
  • • Disaster-management authorities — approve, oversee, and resolve disputes.
  • • Volunteers & organizers — coordinate effort on the ground.

How it works

  1. A citizen submits a request with their location and what they need.
  2. Authorities review it — life-threatening emergencies are auto-approved by the system.
  3. A nearby verified provider accepts and delivers; the citizen confirms completion.

Privacy first

Requests default to Protected: the public sees a request on the map without your name or phone, while assigned responders get your contact through the system. You can raise privacy further at any time.

Pilot

IDRM is launching as a two-district pilot in Hyderabad and Vijayawada, available in English, Hindi, and Telugu.
