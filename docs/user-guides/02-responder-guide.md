*As a responder, IDRM helps you **register your availability, receive and act on alerts, take on tasks, and
share real-time status** — safely and in coordination with others.*

## Get started (first time)

1. Sign in with the account your organisation set up (or register and be verified).
2. Open your **profile** and add your **skills** and **availability**.
3. Check the **map** and **tasks** for activity in your area.

> Ensure your own safety first. Follow your organisation's protocols and any safety instructions in an alert.

## How to…

**Set your availability**
1. Open **Profile → Availability**.
2. Set yourself **Available**, **Busy**, or **Offline**. Keep it current so coordinators can assign the right
   people.

**Receive and act on alerts**
1. Open **Alerts** to see incidents and calls for response near you.
2. Read the details (type, severity, location) and follow safety guidance.

**Take on a task**
1. Open **Tasks** to see what's assigned or available for an incident.
2. Open a task to see the location, priority, and instructions.
3. Update its status as you go: **En route → On site → Completed**.

**Share situational updates**
1. From the incident or task, add an **update** (a short message of what you see or did).
2. Optionally attach a **photo** as evidence. Your update appears on the shared timeline.

**Help with resources**
- If you deliver or move a resource, record its **deployment** so availability stays accurate.

## Reference

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Task** | A unit of work assigned for an incident, with a priority and status. |
| **Task status** | Assigned → En route → On site → Completed (or Cancelled). |
| **Availability** | Whether you can be assigned: Available, Busy, Offline. |
| **Update** | A timeline message you post about an incident/task. |

## Understand

Your live updates and status keep the **shared picture** accurate, so coordinators can direct people and
resources where they're needed most. Prompt, honest status changes are what make the whole response faster and
safer.
