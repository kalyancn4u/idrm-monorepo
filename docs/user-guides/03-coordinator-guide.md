*As a coordinator, IDRM helps you **monitor incidents and resources, coordinate people, allocate resources,
issue directives, and communicate** — all from one shared view.*

## Get started (first time)

1. Sign in with your coordinator account.
2. Open the **dashboard** for an at-a-glance view of active incidents, resources, and alerts.
3. Open the **map** to see incidents, responders, and resources by location.

## How to…

**Monitor and triage incidents**
1. Open **Incidents** (or the dashboard). Filter by **status**, **severity**, **type**, or **area**.
2. Open an incident to review details, its **timeline**, and location.
3. Update its **status** and **severity** as the situation develops.

**Assign tasks**
1. From an incident, open **Tasks → New task**.
2. Set a **title**, **priority**, and **assign** it to a responder.
3. Track progress as responders update the task status.

**Allocate and deploy resources**
1. Open **Resources** to see what's **available** and where.
2. From an incident, create a **deployment** to send a resource; mark it **returned** afterwards.
3. Availability updates automatically so you always see what's free.

**Communicate**
1. Raise an **alert** for an incident and choose the channel (email, SMS, push, in-app).
2. Post **updates** to keep responders and stakeholders informed. Communications are logged.

**Monitor and report**
- Use the **dashboard** and **reports** to watch response times, resolution rates, and resource use, and to
  brief stakeholders.

## Reference

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Incident status** | Reported → Verified → In-Progress → Resolved → Closed. |
| **Deployment** | Sending a resource to an incident (and its return). |
| **Alert** | A message raised for an incident, sent on chosen channels. |
| **Dashboard** | The live summary of incidents, resources, and KPIs. |

## Understand

Coordination is IDRM's heart: you turn incoming reports into **assigned action** and keep everyone working
from the **same picture** (Poster 7, steps 3–6). Clear, timely assignments and communication are what reduce
response time and duplication. Your actions are recorded for accountability
([`../05-security.md`](../05-security.md)).
