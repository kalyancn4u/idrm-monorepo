*As a citizen, IDRM helps you **report incidents, request help, receive alerts, and give feedback** — so the
right people can respond quickly.*

## Get started (first time)

1. Open IDRM in your browser (or the mobile app when available).
2. **Create an account** with your email and a password, then verify your email.
3. Sign in. You'll see the **map** and a menu for reporting and alerts.
4. Allow location access so your reports can be placed accurately.

> Your safety comes first. In a life-threatening emergency, also contact local emergency services directly.

## How to…

**Report an incident or hazard**
1. Tap **Report**.
2. Choose the **type** (e.g. flood, fire, medical) and **severity**.
3. Confirm the **location** on the map (or use your current location).
4. Add a short **description** (what's happening, how many people affected).
5. Optionally attach a **photo**. Tap **Submit** — you'll get a reference number.

**Request assistance**
1. From your incident (or **Request help**), describe what you need (rescue, food, shelter, water, medical).
2. Submit. You can **track its status** as responders act on it.

**Receive and act on alerts**
1. Open **Alerts** to see warnings and updates for your area.
2. Follow any safety instructions included in the alert.
3. Set your **notification preferences** (email / SMS / in-app) in your profile.

**Track your report**
- Open **My reports** to see status changes (submitted → in progress → resolved) and any messages.

**Give feedback**
- After help is provided, you may be asked to **confirm** and leave a short **rating** — this improves future
  response.

## Reference

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reference number** | The ID for your report — quote it when following up. |
| **Status** | Where your report is: submitted, in progress, resolved. |
| **Severity** | How urgent: critical, high, medium, low. |
| **Alert** | A safety warning or update sent to you. |

## Understand

IDRM shows your report on a shared **map** that responders and coordinators see in real time. The more
accurate your location and description, the faster and better the response. Your personal data is protected
and shared only with those coordinating your help (see privacy in [`../05-security.md`](../05-security.md)).
